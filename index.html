Nature & wildlife

Nature · Ticket · 2 h

Herrenhausen Gardens

~1.6 km · tram 4 or 5, 10 min

Baroque geometry at full scale — 50 hectares of clipped hedge, fountains that actually run, and the Grotto that Niki de Saint Phalle lined with mirrors and glass. The single best hour you can spend outdoors here.

Getting there
Tram 4 or 5 from Universität to Herrenhäuser Gärten, then three minutes on foot. Herrenhäuser Str. 4, 30419 Hannover.
Good for
A slow walk, a conversation away from the badge, plant people.
Worth knowing
Ticket at the gate. The Great Garden and the Berggarten have separate entrances, and closing time shifts with the season — check before you set off.

Nature · Ticket · 1 h

SEA LIFE Hannover

~1.5 km · tram 4 or 5, 10 min · indoors

An aquarium tucked in beside the gardens: tropical tanks, sharks, sea turtles and a tunnel you walk through. The obvious answer to a wet afternoon, and it pairs neatly with Herrenhausen next door.

Getting there
Same tram stop as Herrenhausen. Herrenhäuser Str. 4a, 30419 Hannover.
Good for
Rain, jet lag, anyone who has brought family along.
Worth knowing
Tickets are usually cheaper booked online than at the door, and midday is the busiest hour.

Nature · Free · 1–2 h

Maschsee

~3.2 km · 15 min by tram

The city's front garden — a lake in the middle of town with a flat path all the way round. The loop is about six kilometres: an hour at a walk, twenty minutes on a bike. Rowing boats, a passenger ferry, ice cream, and beer on the north shore.

Getting there
Tram towards the centre, then a short walk south past the Neues Rathaus. Start at the northern tip by the Sprengel Museum. 30169 Hannover.
Good for
A run before breakfast, an unhurried walk with a co-author, sunset.
Worth knowing
Costs nothing and never closes. The north end has the cafés and boat hire; the south end is quieter.

Nature · Free · Open-ended

Eilenriede city forest

Western edge ~2 km · Waldstation ~4 km

Twice the size of Central Park, and it begins a short walk east of the venue. Centuries-old trees, streams, sunbathing lawns, fitness trails and the Waldstation forest-education centre. Walk in on any path and you stop hearing the city within minutes.

Getting there
Head east on foot for the nearest edge, or take tram 3 or 7 a few stops. The Waldstation sits deeper in, at Am Lindenberge. 30161 Hannover.
Good for
Clearing your head between sessions, a morning run, an unplanned hour.
Worth knowing
Free and never closed. Paths are well signposted but the forest is genuinely large — keep a map handy if you leave the main routes.

Nature · Ticket · Half a day

Erlebnis-Zoo Hannover

~3 km · 20 min by tram and bus

One of the most-copied zoos in Europe: animals in built worlds — Yukon Bay, Zambezi, Gorilla Mountain — rather than enclosures. It is bigger than it looks from the map, so give it a proper afternoon.

Getting there
Tram towards the centre then bus 128 or 134 to Zoo. Adenauerallee 1, 30175 Hannover.
Good for
A long free afternoon, anyone travelling with children.
Worth knowing
The priciest ticket on this list, and last entry is well before closing — start early rather than after lunch.

Sports & action

Sport · Ticket · 2 h

Beta Boulderhalle

~600 m · 8 min walk · indoors

The nearest thing on this list to the venue, and the easiest to organise on a whim. Bouldering means climbing at jumping height — no ropes, no partner needed. Routes run from complete-beginner to genuinely hard.

Getting there
Eight minutes on foot south of the Welfenschloss. Weidendamm 2, 30167 Hannover.
Good for
Burning off a day of sitting; a low-effort group activity for four to eight people.
Worth knowing
Shoes are rentable at the desk — bring sports clothes and that is all. Evenings after work are the busiest window.
Directions Official site ↗
Sport · Free to watch · 30 min

Leine-Welle

~1.4 km · 18 min walk

A standing wave built into the river in the middle of the old town, right beside the state parliament. River surfers take it in turns while the rest of the city watches from the bridge. Easily the strangest ten minutes you can add to a walk into town — and free.

Getting there
Walk south along the Leine into the Altstadt. Schloßstraße, 30159 Hannover.
Good for
A detour on the way to dinner; watching, mostly.
Worth knowing
Watching from the bridges costs nothing. Actually surfing needs a slot booked in advance, and the wave only runs when river conditions allow — check the site the same day.
Directions Official site ↗
Sport · Ticket · Book ahead

NeonGolf Hannover

~1.1 km · 15 min walk · indoors

Eighteen holes of blacklit mini-golf played in 3D glasses, where the walls appear to move. Gloriously silly, entirely weatherproof, and about the most reliable icebreaker there is for a group who have only just met.

Getting there
Fifteen minutes on foot towards Steintor. Heiligerstraße 15–16, 30159 Hannover.
Good for
Groups of four to twelve; rain; the awkward first evening.
Worth knowing
Book a slot in advance for anything larger than a couple of people — evenings fill up.
Directions Official site ↗
Sport · Ticket · 2 h

Jump One Hannover

~1.2 km · 16 min walk · indoors

A warehouse full of trampolines in Hainholz: jumping zones, dodgeball courts, a ninja-warrior course and airbags for the ambitious. Exhausting in the best way, and completely indifferent to the weather.

Getting there
Sixteen minutes on foot north of campus, or two tram stops. Voltastraße 4, 30165 Hannover.
Good for
Rainy days, competitive people, undoing a day in a lecture hall.
Worth knowing
Grip socks are compulsory and sold at reception. Sessions are sold in fixed blocks — check times before you walk over.
Directions Official site ↗
Sport · Ticket · Booking required

HI-Score

~3.8 km · 20 min by tram · indoors

Germany's largest video-game museum: a thousand square metres, fifty years of hardware, and more than a hundred consoles and arcade cabinets all switched on and free to play once you are inside.

Heads up: open Friday to Sunday only — so this is a before-or-after-the-conference plan, not a Tuesday-evening one.
Getting there
Tram south-east, about twenty minutes door to door. Anna-Zammert-Straße 28, 30171 Hannover.
Opening
Friday 16:00–23:45 · Saturday 12:00–23:45 · Sunday 14:00–17:45. Friday and Saturday evening slots are 18+.
Worth knowing
Admission runs in fixed four-hour slots and an online reservation is mandatory — nothing at the door.

Food & drink

Three of these are inside five minutes of the venue, which is either very convenient or a problem, depending on your Wednesday-morning talk.

Pub · Cheap · No booking

Extrakt

~200 m · 3 min walk

A corner pub three minutes from the Welfenschloss, decorated by accident over about thirty years and beloved by students for it. Pizza, burgers, baked potatoes, cheap beer, no hurry. There is no website, which tells you most of what you need to know.

Getting there
Straight out of the main building and left. Hahnenstraße 3, 30167 Hannover.
Good for
The unplanned post-session pint; a long table of eight; talking rather than looking.
Worth knowing
Cash is welcome and sometimes preferred. No reservations — just turn up early if you are a crowd.

Pub · Cheap · No booking

Destille

~220 m · 3 min walk

The archetypal German student pub, essentially unchanged since the 1970s: dark wooden tables, left-alternative posters, regional beer poured properly and hearty food at fair prices. Two minutes further than Extrakt and a different century.

Getting there
Three minutes west of the venue. Im Moore 3, 30167 Hannover.
Good for
Dinner that does not need planning; a quiet corner for four.
Worth knowing
Kitchen hours are shorter than bar hours — eat earlier than you think.

Bar · Cheap · Late

Santana Cocktailbar

~270 m · 4 min walk

The Nordstadt's cocktail institution: an enormous list, student prices, low light and a room that is always slightly too full. Four minutes from the venue and open long after the posters come down.

Note: smoking is permitted indoors.
Getting there
Four minutes north-west of the venue. Rehbockstraße 38A, 30167 Hannover.

Beach bar · Cheap · Outdoors

Strandleben

~1.3 km · 17 min walk

Sand, deck chairs and a bar where the Ihme meets the Leine. Shoes off, drink in hand, sun going down over the water — the closest Hannover gets to a holiday, seventeen minutes from your last session.

Getting there
Walk south-west along the river. Weddigenufer 25, 30167 Hannover.
Good for
The Tuesday evening if the weather holds; sunset; twenty people who want to spread out.
Worth knowing
Entirely weather-dependent and seasonal — check that it is open before you march everyone over.

Beer garden · Cheap · Outdoors

Waterloo Biergarten

~1.9 km · 24 min walk or 10 by tram

The classic: gravel underfoot, tall trees overhead, long benches you share with strangers, and cold beer with something fried. Big enough to absorb a conference-sized group without a reservation.

Getting there
Tram towards the centre and walk down past the Waterloo column. Waterloostraße 1, 30169 Hannover.
Good for
A big, informal group; the first warm evening; walking on to the Maschsee afterwards.
Worth knowing
Open-air, so rain moves the plan indoors. Sport is often on the screens, which is either a feature or a warning.
Directions Official site ↗
Tavern · Mid · Reserve for groups

Bavarium

~1.9 km · 10 min by tram · indoors

Dark wood, long tables, schnitzel that overhangs the plate, pork knuckle for the brave and Bavarian beer by the litre. If a visiting colleague asks for "proper German food", this is where you take them.

Getting there
City centre, near Aegidientorplatz. Windmühlenstraße 6, 30159 Hannover.
Good for
A sit-down dinner with six to twelve people; first-time visitors to Germany.
Worth knowing
Reserve if you are more than four — it fills in the evening. Vegetarians are catered for, but the menu is unapologetically meat-led.
Directions Official site ↗
Bar · Mid · Loud

Cheers Hannover

~1.3 km · 17 min walk · indoors

Sports bar and karaoke room in one, in the middle of town. Burgers, nachos, screens, and a regular karaoke night that has ended more than one academic reputation. Go if the group wants noise rather than conversation.

Getting there
Seventeen minutes on foot towards Steintor. Goseriede 4, 30159 Hannover.
Good for
The last night; karaoke volunteers; anyone who wants a live match on.
Worth knowing
Check which nights karaoke runs before you promise anyone a stage.

Welfengarten 1, 30167 Hannover — the Welfenschloss, the main building with the lion out front. Stadtbahn stop Universität, lines 4 and 5, is directly outside.

Pharmacies rotate a night-and-weekend duty roster; the nearest open one is listed in the window of any closed Apotheke. Note that most shops and supermarkets close on Sundays.

Wifi

eduroam covers the campus — set it up at your home institution before you travel and it will simply connect. If you have no eduroam account, ask at the registration desk.

Getting around town

Twelve Stadtbahn lines run through the two central hubs, Kröpcke and Hauptbahnhof, frequently and step-free. Buses fill the gaps, and the SprintH express lines reach the wider region. The whole city sits inside GVH tariff zone A, so one zone-A ticket covers anything in this guide. If you already hold a Deutschlandticket, it is valid here.

ÜSTRA timetables ↗ GVH tickets ↗

Hannover is flat and genuinely built for cycling: wide, often red-paved lanes beside the main roads, and twelve signposted velo-routes running star-fashion into the centre away from car traffic. Guarded bike parking with repair services sits at the main station. E-scooters and e-bikes (Tier/Dott, Lime, Bolt) are free-floating across the city, and stadtmobil car-sharing has stations everywhere; Uber and Bolt both operate.

Arriving

Hannover Airport is about 25 minutes from the centre by S-Bahn, every half hour. From the Hauptbahnhof the venue is 2 km — one tram ride or a 25-minute walk. Coming from outside Europe, Frankfurt often has better connections: switch to a direct ICE at the airport's long-distance station.
