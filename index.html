Sights & nature

Nature · Ticket · 2 h

Herrenhausen Gardens

~1.7 km · tram 4 or 5, 10 min

Originally constructed at the end of the 17th century as part of the nobility's summer residence. The gardens feature Baroque geometry at full scale — 50 hectares of clipped hedge and fountains. It features many different areas, like the golden figures, the orange parterre, or smaller bosquet gardens. Also features the Grotto that Niki de Saint Phalle lined with mirrors and glass.

Getting there
Tram 4 or 5 from Universität to Herrenhäuser Gärten, then three minutes on foot. Herrenhäuser Str. 4, 30419 Hannover.
Directions Official site ↗
Nature · Ticket · 1–2 h

Berggarten

~1.8 km · tram 4 or 5, 10 min

Opposite the Herrenhausen Gardens and next to the SEA LIFE. Green spaces here are less formally laid out but just as beautiful, featuring many local or exotic plants. Here you will also find greenhouses, including the largest orchid exhibition in Europe. You can either get combined tickets for the Herrenhausen Gardens and the Berggarten or tickets for the Berggarten only.

Getting there
Tram 4 or 5 from Universität to Herrenhäuser Gärten. The entrance is on the north side of the street, across from the Great Garden. Herrenhäuser Str. 4, 30419 Hannover.
Directions Official site ↗
Nature · Ticket · 1 h

SEA LIFE Hannover

~1.7 km · tram 4 or 5, 10 min · indoors

An aquarium next to the Berggarten: more than 2,500 animals from 160 species, including Cuban crocodiles, rays, and sea turtles. Also featuring an underwater tunnel. A good answer to a wet afternoon, and it pairs neatly with Herrenhausen next door.

Getting there
Same tram stop as Herrenhausen. Herrenhäuser Str. 4a, 30419 Hannover.
Directions Official site ↗
Nature · Free · 1–2 h

Maschsee

~2.8 km · 15 min by tram

A 2.4 km long lake in the middle of the city and a very popular leisure area. There is a circular walk that goes all the way around the lake (approx. 6 km). Walk, jog, or rent a pedal boat — or simply sit down and enjoy the evening.

Getting there
Tram toward the center, then a short walk south past the Neues Rathaus. Start at the northern tip by the Sprengel Museum. 30169 Hannover.
Directions More info ↗
Nature · Free · Open-ended

Eilenriede city forest

Eastern edge ~2 km · Waldstation ~4.9 km

Hannover's 640-hectare forest and one of the largest city forests in Europe. A great way to escape the noise of the city.

Getting there
Head east on foot for the nearest edge, or take tram 3 or 7 a few stops. The Waldstation sits deeper in, at Kleestraße 81, 30625 Hannover.
Directions More info ↗
Nature · Ticket · Half a day

Erlebnis-Zoo Hannover

~3.6 km · 20 min by tram

The zoo in Hannover is located in the middle of the Eilenriede. It is home to more than 150 species, divided into several thematic areas, such as Yukon Bay, Zambezi, and Afi Mountain. The whole park is very family-friendly, featuring several playgrounds, a boat trip, and an area for petting goats and sheep. The zoo closes at 18:30, so start your day early.

Getting there
Tram 11 runs directly to Zoo. Adenauerallee 3, 30175 Hannover.
Directions Official site ↗
Landmark · Free lobby · Dome lift

Neues Rathaus

~2.2 km · 12 min by tram

Hannover's town hall. You can freely enter the lobby during the opening hours and explore the four city models showcasing Hannover as it has changed over the centuries. For a few euros, you can also take the curved dome lift and get great panoramic views of Hannover.

Getting there
Tram toward the center, then a short walk through the Maschpark. Trammplatz 2, 30159 Hannover.
Directions Official site ↗

Sport · Ticket · Booking required

HI-Score

~3.8 km · 20 min by tram · indoors

Germany's largest video-game museum: a thousand square meters, fifty years of hardware, and more than a hundred consoles and arcade cabinets all switched on and free to play once you are inside.

Heads up: open Friday to Sunday only — so this is a before-or-after-the-conference plan, not a Tuesday-evening one. Admission runs in fixed four-hour slots and an online reservation is mandatory.
Getting there
Tram south-east, about twenty minutes door to door. Anna-Zammert-Straße 28, 30171 Hannover.

Ice cream · Takeaway · 30 min

Leine Eis

~1.5 km · 20 min walk

One of the really good ice cream parlors in Hannover. They serve handmade ice cream, often in unusual flavors, although it is a bit pricey. The shop offers only very few seating options. However, you can walk to the river Leine, which is only a few meters away. From there, you have a good view of the Leine-Welle, a standing wave built into the river. On a good day, you can watch the surfers riding the wave.

Getting there
Walk south along the Leine into the Altstadt — it sits a few steps from the Leine-Welle. Klostergang 1, 30159 Hannover.

Getting around town

Twelve Stadtbahn lines run through the two central hubs, Kröpcke and Aegidientorplatz, frequently and mostly step-free. Buses fill the gaps, and the SprintH express lines reach the wider region. The whole city sits inside GVH tariff zone A, so one zone-A ticket covers anything in this guide. If you already hold a Deutschlandticket, it is valid here. When you pick up your badge from the registration desk, you can also pick up a free ÜSTRA ticket for the duration of the conference.

ÜSTRA timetables ↗ GVH tickets ↗

Hannover is flat and can be explored by bike: wide, often red-paved lanes beside the main roads, and twelve signposted velo-routes running star-fashion into the center. E-scooters and e-bikes are free-floating across the city; Uber and Bolt both operate.

Arriving

Hannover Airport is about 25 minutes from the center by S-Bahn, every half hour. From the Hauptbahnhof the venue is 2 km, one tram ride or a 25-minute walk. Coming from outside Europe, Frankfurt often has better connections: switch to a direct ICE at the airport's long-distance station.
